2. In `planFacts.ts` add `export function parseVesting(text: string | null | undefined): { kind: 'immediate' } | { kind: 'schedule'; byYear: number[] } | null`. `byYear[n]` = percent vested after n full years, from year 0 until the first 100.
   - `immediate`: text says 100% and "immediate"/"immediately"/"right away" and names no years.
   - Cliff: `/(\d+)[- ]year cliff/i` or `/100%\s*(after|at)\s*(\d+)\s*years?/i` with no other percentages → 0 until that year, then 100.
   - Graded: only when the text lists explicit pairs, either `/(\d+)%\s*(?:after|at)?\s*(\d+)\s*(?:years?)?/` or `/(\d+)\s*years?\s*[:=-]?\s*(\d+)%/`. Fill missing earlier years with 0, require percentages that never go down and end at 100, at most 10 years.
   - Anything else, including a bare "6-year graded" with no percentages: `null`. Never guess a schedule.
3. Move the sample plans from `scripts/check-answers.ts` into `scripts/samples.ts` (export `samples` and `mk`); `check-answers.ts` imports them, same results.
4. Add to `scripts/check-answers.ts` a `parseVesting` table that must all pass:
   `"100% immediately vested"` → immediate; `"Immediate 100% vesting"` → immediate; `"3-year cliff"` → [0,0,0,100]; `"100% after 3 years of service"` → [0,0,0,100]; `"20% after 2 years, 40% after 3 years, 60% after 4, 80% after 5, 100% after 6"` → [0,0,20,40,60,80,100]; `"2 years: 20%; 3 years: 40%; 4 years: 60%; 5 years: 80%; 6 years: 100%"` → same; `"6-year graded"` → null; `"Vesting per the plan document"` → null; `""` → null; `"40% after 2 years, 20% after 3"` → null.
